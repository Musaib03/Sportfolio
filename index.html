About Me

Bridging Theory & Real-World Impact

I am Sania Maqbool, a graduate of a 5-year Integrated M.A. in Economics program. My journey through economics has been shaped by a deep curiosity about how markets, institutions, and policies interact to shape human well-being.

From microeconomic theory to macroeconomic policy analysis, I've built a comprehensive toolkit that allows me to approach complex economic questions with both rigor and creativity. I am particularly drawn to development economics, behavioral economics, and the use of quantitative methods to evaluate policy interventions.

Outside academia, I enjoy translating economic insights into accessible narratives — because good economics should speak to everyone.
